Cookie Policy

Last updated: 2026-05-11

This Cookie Policy explains how https://datacloudhero.com/ and https://blog.datacloudhero.com/ (“we”, “us”, or “our”) use cookies and similar technologies when you visit our websites.

By using our website, you can choose whether to accept, reject, or customize the use of non-essential cookies. You can change your cookie preferences at any time through our cookie settings panel.

1. What are cookies?​

Cookies are small text files that are stored on your device when you visit a website. They help websites function properly, improve user experience, remember your preferences, and provide analytics or marketing information.

We may also use similar technologies, such as pixels, tags, scripts, local storage, and tracking technologies.

2. How we use cookies​

We use cookies to:

  • operate and secure our website;
  • remember your preferences;
  • understand how visitors use our website;
  • improve our services and user experience;
  • measure the performance of our content and marketing campaigns;
  • provide relevant advertising, where applicable.

Some cookies are necessary for the website to function and cannot be disabled through our cookie settings. Other cookies are optional and will only be used with your consent.

3. Types of cookies we use​

Strictly necessary cookies​

These cookies are required for the website to work properly. They enable core features such as page navigation, security, login sessions, and access to protected areas of the website.

These cookies do not require your consent.

Functional cookies​

These cookies allow us to remember your preferences, such as language, region, or other settings, to provide a more personalized experience.

Analytics cookies​

Analytics cookies help us understand how visitors interact with our website. They allow us to collect information such as pages visited, time spent on the website, traffic sources, and error reports.

This information helps us improve the performance and usability of our website.

Marketing cookies​

Marketing cookies may be used to deliver relevant advertisements, measure advertising performance, and track user activity across websites.

These cookies are only used if you give your consent.

Third-party cookies​

Some cookies may be placed by third-party services that we use on our website, such as analytics providers, advertising networks, payment providers, video platforms, or customer support tools.

These third parties may process your data according to their own privacy and cookie policies.

5. Managing your cookie preferences​

You can manage your cookie preferences at any time by clicking the “Cookie Settings” link or button available on our website.

You may choose to:

  • accept all cookies;
  • reject non-essential cookies;
  • customize your cookie preferences by category.

Please note that disabling some cookies may affect the functionality or performance of the website.

6. Browser settings​

You can also control or delete cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to block cookies, delete existing cookies, or receive a warning before cookies are stored.

Please refer to your browser’s help section for more information.

If you disable cookies in your browser, some parts of our website may not function properly.
